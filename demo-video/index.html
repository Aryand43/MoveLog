MoveMate — a voice agent for movers and packers
A packing crew logs a hundred boxes a day on paper.
Ops finds out what's inside them tomorrow.
And when something is already damaged, nobody knows whose fault it is until the claim lands.
MoveMate is one agent that lives in three places.
The packer's earbuds. The ops team's Telegram group. The customer's private chat.
He taps start. The phone goes in his pocket and stays there.
"Box twelve, kitchen. Glasses, the blender, two chopping boards. Fragile."
"Twelve, kitchen, three items, fragile." — 1.4 seconds, no screen touched
"Actually put the phone chargers in that one too."
A mid-sentence correction, handled. This is a conversation, not a form.
"Where did the router go?"
"Box A9, study." — every box logged today is queryable, by voice, mid-lift
"The oak dresser has a scratch on the left side."
"Noted — that one's on the survey." — pre-existing damage, recognised, no escalation
"The sofa has a tear on the back cushion."
"Not on the survey. Grab a photo."
He taps once. The photo goes to GPT‑5.6 Luna — not into the voice session.
"Six-inch tear, minor, looks new. Logging it."
Ops never opened a dashboard. The card came to where they already are.
Photo, assessment, survey verdict — and three buttons that actually resolve the case.
Priya taps Wrap and load.
"Ops says wrap and load. Claim opened." — spoken into his earbuds, in the same session
Twenty-seven seconds, defect to decision. Nobody typed. Nobody put the box down.
At handover the customer gets a deep link to the same bot — scoped to their move only.
"Did my TV get packed?"
Every answer comes from the same tools the packer used. One source of truth.
No chatbox could carry that round trip alone.
